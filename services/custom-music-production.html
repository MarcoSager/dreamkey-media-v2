Our Process

From Brief to Track

01

Creative Brief

Tell us your brand personality, the mood you want to evoke, reference tracks you like, and how the music will be used.

02

Concept & Composition

We compose 2-3 initial directions using AI music models guided by our creative team. You choose a direction to develop.

03

Refinement

We refine the chosen track based on your feedback — adjusting tempo, instrumentation, dynamics, and feel until it's perfect.

04

Final Delivery

Delivered in WAV and MP3 formats, with stems if needed. Full ownership rights transferred to you — no royalties, ever.
